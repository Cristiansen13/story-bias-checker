4 · Proposed architecture

From story premises to a per-model report

1 Probe bank

89 premises in 19 categories; gender, origin, age and roles left open

2 Generation

The same premises for every model; one prompt and sampling setup

3 Clean & store

Strip reasoning and titles, deduplicate; SQLite keeps every annotation round

6 Report

Per-model rates with 95% intervals, per category and per premise

5 Classifier

Semi-supervised, with evidence sentences; lexical and demographic analyzers

4 Blind annotation

17 labels, casting criteria C1–C4, exact evidence quotes, repeated stories

Review loop (5 back to 4): flags are queued by uncertainty; accepted or rejected flags become new labels

The architecture is a pipeline. 89 premises in 19 categories leave every attribute open. Every model gets the same prompt and sampling. Outputs are cleaned and stored with every annotation round. Blind annotators apply 17 labels and explicit casting criteria, quoting the evidence. A semi-supervised classifier and lexical analyzers learn from the labels, and the report gives per-model rates with intervals. The orange loop is the human in the loop: uncertain flags go back to reviewers and become labels.
