A gap beyond casting

Open-weight 24% (20–29), Claude 16% (13–21), z ≈ 2.3.

Written rules are needed

On any label the gap moves with the annotation round, from 6 to 28 points. With written criteria, two groups agree on casting at κ 0.95.

Size does not help

Qwen3.5 4B, 9B and 27B are at 28%, 26% and 26%, with 70–73% male protagonists and “Elias” in 41–57% of stories.

The classifier is a baseline

On 715 labelled stories, macro-F1 is 0.16 and bias-vs-none accuracy 51%, so its flags still need review.

These are basic experiments to check that the plan works, and they are preliminary: LLM readers stand in for people, with 89 stories per model. Annotation rounds disagree on whether casting alone counts, so the measure to trust is stereotypes beyond casting: 24 percent for the open-weight models against 16 for Claude. That also tells us the casting rule has to be written down, and in a pilot of written criteria two independent groups agreed at kappa 0.95. Larger Qwen models are no cleaner, and Qwen defaults to male heroes and reuses one name, Elias, in about half its stories. The classifier is only a baseline so far.
