A gap beyond casting

Open-weight 26%, Claude 7%: +19 points (95% interval +14 to +24). Casting itself does not differ (−1, −7 to +5).

Family matters more than size

Mistral 49%, OLMo 14%. Qwen3.5 4B, 9B and 27B are at 34%, 24% and 27%.

Different defaults

Qwen3.5: 70–73% male protagonists, “Elias” in 41–57% of stories. OLMo 7B and gpt-oss: 30–33% male.

The classifier is a baseline

Macro-F1 0.28 with an LLM judge; bias-vs-none accuracy 62%, against 60% for always answering “none”.

Preliminary: LLM readers stand in for people and may favour Claude’s stories; Claude’s sampling could not be fixed. Stories read twice agree at κ 0.85.

These are basic experiments to check that the plan works. We read one story per premise and model in one balanced round, so all eleven models are judged together. Beyond casting, open-weight stories are at 26 percent against 7 for Claude, while casting itself does not differ. Family matters more than size: Mistral is highest, OLMo is closest to Claude, and larger Qwen models are not cleaner. Defaults differ too: Qwen favours male heroes and reuses the name Elias, while OLMo 7B and gpt-oss lean female. The classifier is only a baseline so far. All of this is preliminary, because LLM readers stand in for people and may favour Claude's stories.
